## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and engineering hiring managers evaluating Lucas Loepke for new-grad software engineering roles, usually skimming on a laptop or phone between other candidates. (Inferred from the site's stated job search; confirm if other audiences matter.)

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio. Success means a visitor quickly understands what Lucas has shipped and when he can start, and has an obvious way to get in touch.

## Positioning

Three years of SAP rotations (analytics tooling, native iOS, agentic AI), plus independently shipped work with real users: a Steam release, Chrome extensions and hackathon wins. Started out running Minecraft servers that turned a profit.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React, Vite and Tailwind, deployed to GitHub Pages. SPA fallback through `404.html`; `scripts/generate-meta.mjs` bakes route metadata.
- `/commit-art` is a public, featured project and is indexed. Owner decision.

## Brand Commitments

- The site should feel sleek, like an Apple product, and be exciting to scroll. It evolves the original navy design with the bubbles background; oversized type and loud color fields are out.

## Evidence on Hand

- Facts come from `public/Loepke_Resume.pdf` and `src/data/`.
- Steam listing for Cleanup Crew, Chrome Web Store listings for Simple Canvas Tasks and LeetVision.
- Photos: `public/portrait.*`.
- Extension screenshots: `public/projects/`. Do not invent metrics, testimonials or employers.

## Product Principles

- Show the work before describing the person.
- Lead with outcomes; list technology afterwards, as plain text.
- Every claim is something a visitor can check.
- A visitor can always reach Lucas without hunting.
